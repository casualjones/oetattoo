Let's Create

I am learning. I offer collaborative work—low-cost, trade, or exchange.

  • Design consult
  • Aftercare guidance
  • Photos (consent)

Requirements

  • Size: Small to medium (palm-sized to forearm)
  • Placement: Anywhere (no face, no hands)
  • Commitment: You're investing your skin; I'm investing my skill
  • Timeline: 2-4 weeks from consultation to appointment

How It Works

  1. Reserve - Submit your interest & inspiration
  2. Consult - We chat about your geometric vision
  3. Design - I create your custom design
  4. Appointment - We bring it to life
  5. Forever - You carry our collaboration
